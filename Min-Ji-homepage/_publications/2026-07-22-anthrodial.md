Human-like private chat requires more than fluent response generation: a system must preserve persona, relationship, memory, bounded knowledge, medium-specific timing, and a coherent multi-turn arc.

This paper presents **AnthroDial**, a closed-loop framework that treats anthropomorphic dialogue as a joint problem of system architecture, executable evaluation, and diagnostic alignment.
